This view cannot load the board

You are probably looking at a static preview or copy, for example in an agent's built-in browser. It cannot run the board or reach state.js. Open the board in your system browser:

open .wallpainter/index.html

Linux: xdg-open, Windows: start. To show it inside an embedded browser, serve it locally and open http://localhost:8766:

python3 -m http.server 8766 --bind 127.0.0.1 --directory .wallpainter
